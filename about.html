Who We Are

Mike Adams started Mike's Services LLC in 2021 with a simple goal: honest septic and site work to property owners across Central Kansas who needed a contractor they could actually count on to show up, communicate clearly, and do the job right.

Mike's Services is not a large company and that is intentional. When you call, you talk to Mike or someone on his crew directly. Not a call center, not a dispatcher three states away. You get direct answers, honest timelines, and a crew that respects your property and your time.

Since 2021 Mike Adams and his crew have completed projects across Saline, Ottawa, Lincoln, Dickinson, Ellsworth, and Cloud Counties: septic installations for new rural homes, excavation for new construction, land clearing on neglected acreage, building pad prep for shops and barns, and demolition of old structures that had outlived their usefulness. Every job gets the same attention regardless of size.

Mike believes the best advertisement is a satisfied customer who tells their neighbor. That is how most of the work comes in and it is the standard held on every project.

Why Hire Local

Central Kansas ground is specific. Soil conditions, county regulations, frost depths, drainage patterns, and the practical realities of rural property work here are different from what you will find in a city or in another region of the state. A contractor who works this ground regularly understands those differences in a way that someone coming from outside the area simply does not.

Mike Adams knows the counties he works in. He knows the local health departments and their inspection processes. He knows which soil types cause problems for septic drain fields and which ground conditions require extra compaction work on building pads. That local knowledge translates directly into better outcomes for your project.

When you hire a local contractor you also get accountability that a large regional company cannot offer. Mike lives and works in this community. His reputation here matters in a way that goes beyond any single job.

Mike Adams is your neighbor. He takes that seriously.

How Mike Works

Three things that define every project Mike Adams and his crew take on.

Honest Quotes

Mike assesses your site before quoting. You get a real number based on what the job actually requires, not a low number to win the bid followed by change orders. If something changes during the project Mike tells you immediately and explains why.

Clear Communication

You will know when the crew is coming, what they are doing each day, and when they expect to finish. If something comes up that affects the schedule, Mike calls you. He does not leave you wondering what is happening on your own property.

No Subcontractors

The crew that shows up to do your job is Mike's crew. He does not hand your project off to someone else. That means consistent quality, direct accountability, and the assurance that the people doing the work have a stake in doing it right.
