Our Service Area

We are a Central Kansas contractor based out of Salina. Our service area covers a wide radius across the region — from the Smoky Hills to the rolling plains west of Salina. If your property is in or near any of the counties listed below we can almost certainly help. Not sure? Call us and we will give you a straight answer.

Saline County

Our home county. We serve all of Saline County including Salina, Assaria, Gypsum, Brookville, and surrounding rural areas. Septic installation, excavation, land clearing, building pad prep, and demolition services available throughout the county.

Ottawa County

We serve Ottawa County including Minneapolis, Tescott, Bennington, Delphos, and surrounding communities. Full range of site work services available for rural and residential property owners across Ottawa County.

Lincoln County

Lincoln County service including Lincoln, Sylvan Grove, Barnard, and surrounding rural properties. Septic, excavation, land clearing, and site prep for homeowners and agricultural property owners.

Dickinson County

Serving Dickinson County including Abilene, Chapman, Enterprise, and surrounding areas. New construction site prep, septic installation, excavation, and demolition for residential and rural properties.

Ellsworth County

Ellsworth County service including Ellsworth, Kanopolis, Holyrood, and surrounding communities. Site work, land clearing, building pad preparation, and septic services for property owners across the county.

Cloud County

Serving Cloud County including Concordia, Glasco, Jamestown, and surrounding rural areas. Septic installation, excavation, land clearing, and demolition for residential and agricultural properties.
