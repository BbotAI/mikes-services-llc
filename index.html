Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services and when to call.

When do I need septic system work?

Common signs you need septic service include slow drains, soggy spots in your yard, sewage backups, or a strong odor near your tank. New installations are needed for properties without municipal sewer. We recommend regular inspections every 3 to 5 years and tank pumping every 3 to 4 years depending on household size and usage.

What's involved in septic installation?

Septic installation begins with a site evaluation and soil testing. We handle full excavation, tank placement, drain field design, backfill, and final grading. The process requires coordination with your local health department for inspections at key stages. We manage the timeline and inspections so you don't have to.

Why does land need clearing?

Land clearing removes obstacles that prevent productive use of your property. Whether preparing for new construction, improving pastures, removing dead trees, or clearing brush, proper clearing creates usable space and reduces fire hazards. Overgrown properties can hide foundation issues and create pest problems — clearing lets you see and manage your land properly.

What is a building pad and when do I need one?

A building pad is a precisely graded and compacted area of ground prepared to support a structure, driveway, or concrete slab. You need one any time you are building a home, shop, barn, or outbuilding on raw land. Proper pad preparation prevents settling, cracking, and drainage problems down the road. We grade, compact, and verify elevation to your project specs.

Why would I need demolition services?

Demolition is needed when buildings are unsafe, no longer useful, or in the way of new development. Old structures may have foundation issues or environmental concerns that make them unsuitable to repair. Clearing removes these obstacles safely and opens your land for new use.

How do I get started with excavation or trenching work?

Contact us with details about your project — whether it is site prep for new construction, utility trenching, grading, or foundation work. We will discuss your needs, assess the site, and provide a clear quote. Many projects can be scheduled quickly depending on seasonal conditions and equipment availability.
