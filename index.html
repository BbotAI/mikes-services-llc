Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services and when to call.

When do I need septic system work?

Common signs you need septic service include slow drains, soggy spots in your yard, sewage backups, or a strong odor near your tank. New installations are needed for properties without municipal sewer. We recommend regular inspections every 3-5 years and tank pumping every 3-4 years depending on household size and usage.

What's involved in septic installation?

Septic installation begins with a site evaluation and soil testing. We handle full excavation, tank placement, drain field design, backfill, and final grading. The process requires coordination with your local health department for inspections at key stages. We manage the timeline and inspections so you don't have to.

Why does land need clearing?

Land clearing removes obstacles that prevent productive use of your property. Whether preparing for new construction, improving pastures, removing dead trees, or clearing brush, proper clearing creates usable space and reduces fire hazards. Over-grown properties can hide foundation issues and create pest problems—clearing lets you see and manage your land properly.

What causes overgrowth and how is it managed?

Overgrowth happens when trees, brush, and vegetation spread unchecked over time. Management depends on your goals: selective clearing keeps valuable trees, complete clearing removes everything for a fresh start, and pasture clearing maintains grazing land. We assess your property and recommend the best approach for your situation.

Why would I need demolition services?

Demolition is needed when buildings are unsafe, no longer useful, or in the way of new development. Old houses may have structural damage, foundation issues, or environmental concerns (asbestos, lead) that make them unsuitable to repair. Clearing removes these obstacles safely and opens your land for new use.

When should I consider demolishing an old structure?

Consider demolition when repair costs exceed replacement value, the building is unsafe or code-non-compliant, or you want to redevelop the land. We assess conditions and help you understand whether repair or demolition makes financial and practical sense for your property.

How do I get started with excavation work?

Contact us with details about your project—whether it's site prep for new construction, grading, utility work, or foundation work. We'll discuss your needs, assess the site, and provide a clear quote. Many projects can be scheduled quickly depending on seasonal conditions and equipment availability.

What areas do you service?

We serve Tescott and Central Kansas including Saline, Ottawa, Lincoln, Dickinson, Ellsworth, and Cloud Counties. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, call or submit a quote request—we may be able to help even outside these counties.
